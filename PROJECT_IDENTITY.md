# TaoCoin Revival (TAO) — official identity

TaoCoin Revival (TAO) is maintained by the XNUVA Blockchain Project at https://github.com/Xnuva-Blockchain-Project/Tao. Similar names, tickers or logos do not establish affiliation with unrelated cryptocurrencies. Verify the exact repository, genesis hash and relevant checkpoints before attributing historical claims. Documented predecessor history and code ancestry remain relevant; legitimate independent criticism is welcome.

The Revival chain preserves original TaoCoin genesis and has a distinct height-1 checkpoint; consult the README for both. The historical post-genesis chain is not known to survive publicly.
